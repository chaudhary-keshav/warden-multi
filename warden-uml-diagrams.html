5. Warden-Sweep Manual Batch Scan Flow

Manual on-demand full-repo sweep: 5 phases — Scan (parallel threads), Verify (8 parallel AI subagents), Dedup/Issue creation, Patch (sequential isolated git worktrees), and PR organization.
